# PLACEHOLDER

## Description

A vanilla+ building and utility expansion for Minecraft's honeycomb and wax. **Honeycomb+** expands the honeycomb palette with 25 new architectural, decorative, glass, and lighting blocks—from Polished, Cut, and Brick sets to Honeyed Glass and redstone-toggleable Honeycomb Lamps—alongside **Fine Wax** for waxing copper blocks and Copper Golems.

Includes automatic Recipe Book discovery whenever you obtain honeycomb, honey bottles, honeycomb blocks, Fine Wax, or **Honeycomb+** blocks, plus a dedicated Creative Tab.

## Features

### Honeycomb Building Sets
*   **Base Honeycomb**: Honeycomb Stairs, Honeycomb Slab, and Honeycomb Wall.
*   **Polished Honeycomb**: Polished Honeycomb, Stairs, Slab, and Wall.
*   **Cut Honeycomb**: Cut Honeycomb, Stairs, Slab, and Wall.
*   **Honeycomb Bricks**: Honeycomb Bricks, Stairs, Slab, and Wall.

### Architectural & Decorative Blocks
*   **Sculpted**: Chiseled Honeycomb and Honeycomb Pillar.
*   **Lattice & Entryways**: Honeycomb Grate, Honeycomb Bars, Honeycomb Door, and Honeycomb Trapdoor.

### Honeyed Glass & Lighting
*   **Honeyed Glass & Honeyed Glass Pane**: Translucent honey-infused glass with Honey Block-style sticky sliding, fall damage reduction, and movement drag.
*   **Honeycomb Lantern**: Hanging or standing lantern emitting light level 15.
*   **Honeycomb Lamp**: Redstone-toggleable lamp (Copper Bulb behavior) emitting light level 15 when lit.

### Fine Wax & Copper Utility
*   **Fine Wax**: Obtained by smelting, smoking, or campfire-cooking Honeycomb.
*   **Copper Waxing**: Can be used to wax copper blocks in-world, via Dispensers, or through crafting recipes, and can also wax Copper Golems.

### In-Game Discovery
*   Automatic Recipe Book unlocks triggered when collecting Honeycomb, Honey Bottles, Honeycomb Blocks, Fine Wax, or **Honeycomb+** blocks.

## Requirements

*   Minecraft `26.3`

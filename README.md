# Honeycomb+ (`honeycombplus`)

A vanilla+ building and utility expansion for Minecraft's honeycomb and wax. **Honeycomb+** expands the honeycomb palette with 25 new architectural, decorative, glass, and lighting blocks—plus **Fine Wax** for waxing copper blocks and Copper Golems.

## Features

* **Honeycomb Building Sets:** Stairs, Slabs, and Walls for Base, Polished, Cut, and Brick Honeycomb variants.
* **Architectural & Decorative:** Chiseled Honeycomb, Honeycomb Pillar, Honeycomb Grate, Honeycomb Bars, Honeycomb Door, and Honeycomb Trapdoor.
* **Honeyed Glass & Lighting:** Translucent Honeyed Glass & Panes (with honey-block sliding/fall-damage reduction), Honeycomb Lantern, and redstone-toggleable Honeycomb Lamp.
* **Fine Wax & Copper Utility:** Smelt/smoke/campfire-cook Honeycomb into Fine Wax to wax copper blocks (in-world, via Dispensers, or in crafting) and Copper Golems.
* **In-Game Discovery:** Automatic Recipe Book unlocks when obtaining honeycomb, honey bottles, honeycomb blocks, Fine Wax, or **Honeycomb+** blocks, plus a dedicated Creative Tab.

## Requirements

* **Minecraft:** `26.3`
* **Fabric Loader:** `>=0.19.5`
* **Fabric API**
